Idea: A real-time "attractivness-score" based on statistics of the structure of the human face.

User Story (for now)

- User can open up a special camera state that instantly starts tracking a face (when in view) and
collects data about the structure of the face
- Validatation sequence for scanning
- When scan complete, there will a page that shows their score and the breakdown of the score (like
all of the math that went into calculating the score) of various aspects of the face, like symmetry,
proportions, etc. of the face, then give you a full score out of 100.

Tech Stack

- Flutter (Desktop)
- MediaPipe
- TensorFlow Lite
- Math

Pages:

- Pre Scan Page
- Scan Page
- Post Scan Page
